Join us for The D&D Halloween Spooktacular!

We've got games, prizes and costume contests!

When?

Sunday 25th October, 4PM - 9PM Server Time (That's ??? in your local time)

Where?

We meet at the FC House

What?

We've got a packed schedule of games and competitions for you guys throughout the evening, with lots of amazing prizes to give away!

Costume Contests

We've got 3 Halloween Costume Contests we'll be judging on the day:

  • TV & Video Game Characters: Dress as your favourite TV or Video Game Character
  • Leadership Mimicry: Dress up as one of the FC Leads, creativity over accuracy is highly encouraged!
  • Wildcard: Dress up as anything you please. Go Wild!

To give everyone a fair chance, creativity beats accuracy here - don't expect to win just because you bought a ready-to-go character glam off the Square Enix Store!

The costume contests will be held between games with 15 minute prep time at the start for anyone who wants to enter the contests to finish off their glam, change their hair or just take some deep breaths and prepare to stun us all!

Also, we realise everyone is at different stages of the game and we've had a lot of new players join recently. If anyone needs some Gil to help put together their costume glams, just let us know!

Struggling with glams? Don't know how to setup glamour plates?

Check out this guide on how to start using Glams!

And if you need some ideas, chest out Eorzea Collection for inspiration, ideas on what pieces to use and more!

Games!

We'll also be running a handful of games during the evening:

  • Human Darts: Take aim and jump! Please make sure you have Coerthas Central Highlands unlocked if you're still in ARR because we're going jumping off the Observatorium and aiming to land as close to the specified target as possible.
  • Scavenger Hunt: The witch needs fresh ingredients for her potion. The first to gather them for her will get a delightful reward. But she will know if they've been bought off the marketboard so BEWARE.
  • And a good old game of Hide & Seek to round off the night!
