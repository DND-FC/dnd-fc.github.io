Prizes

The plan

Games, costumes, then more games

We’ll play a game, run a costume competition, then do it all again. The exact order and timings may move around a little on the night, so come along and join in whenever you can.

The full event runs for five hours, but you don’t have to stay for every round.

Things to play

The games

Hide & Seek

Find a clever spot, stay quiet and hope the seeker runs out of time before they spot you.

View the guide

Human Darts

Take aim, make the leap and try to land closer to the target than everyone else.

Sardines

Hide & Seek in reverse: one person hides and everyone searches. Find them, squeeze into their hiding place and try not to give the game away.

Scavenger Hunt

Follow the clues, track down the targets and get back before the other teams.

Dress to impress

Four costume competitions

Costumes are completely optional. If you do enter, we want to see some imagination—simply buying a full Cloud Strife outfit from the Store isn’t going to earn many points.

  • TV Characters

    Recreate someone from live action, animation or anything in between.

  • Game Characters

    Pick a character from any game—not just FFXIV—and put your own glamour together.

  • FC Leadership Team Member

    Choose someone from the photo below and do your best—or worst—impression of their usual look.

  • Wild Card

    No prompt beyond common sense. Bring the strangest, funniest or best-looking idea you have.
