Our regular events

What we run each week varies, but it's generally one of these

Treasure maps

Farming treasure maps from recent expansions with open loot rolls and regular reminders to keep hydrated.

Mount farming

Helping new players and old farm Extreme mounts from Stormblood onwards.

Unsynced, of course.

Hide and seek

A fun low-barrier to entry evening of hide and seek in cities and low-level zones.

View rules

Bi-Colour Gem Farming

Farming FATEs in Dawntrail for Shared FATE Zone Leveling and Bicolor Gemstones farming.

High-end raids

Looking for a challenge? We might be a casual FC but we still have a high-end raid team.
