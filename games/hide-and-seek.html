Hide and seek

Everything you need to sort the parties, hide a few bits of UI and get the game underway.

The general idea

How we play

02

Decide the order

Before the first round, everyone rolls /dice.
The host uses the results to set the seeking order, so you can blame the numbers rather than us.

/dice
01

Split the parties

The host will announce the zone for the current round and split off into a separate party with just them and the current seeker.

03

Hide

The seeker heads over to another zone and promises not to peek. Everyone else gets time to find their best hiding spot, how long they get is up to the host.

Our usual hiding time: 5 minutes
04

Seek

Once the host gives the word, the seeker comes back into the zone and the hunt begins. They normally get ten minutes, but it's ultimately up to the host to decide.

Our usual seeking time: 10 minutes
05

Score the round

Seeker
One point for every player they manage to find.

Still hidden?
You earn one point if time runs out before you're found.

02
Keep it fun

Rules

  1. Stay in the zone

    Stick to the zone and any boundaries the host gives you.

  2. No maps!

    The seeker keeps the map and minimap closed, just to make it a little harder to navigate around.

  3. Make it obvious

    Spotting someone from 50 yalms away does not count. Go up to them, target them and use an emote to make the find official.

  4. Don't snitch

    Once found, you're welcome to follow the seeker around. Just don't interact with, point at or otherwise reveal anyone still hiding.

  5. Pick a spot and commit

    Once the seeker enters the zone, stay in your hiding spot until you're found or the round ends.

03
Don't make it too easy

Hide a few UI bits

Before charging in, the seeker should run these commands in the FFXIV chat box.

We've added some copy buttons to make life easier.

Nameplates

Nameplates only appear after you target someone. Much fairer.

/nameplatedisp all 3

Minimap

No helpful minimap dots pointing towards nearby players.

/hud "Minimap" off
04
After the game

Put everything back

Finished seeking? You can turn your UI back on now.

Nameplates

Set all player nameplates to show all the time again.

/nameplatedisp all 1

Minimap

Bring the minimap and its helpful dots back.

/hud "Minimap" on

Nameplate setting 1 means "Always." If that is not your usual setting, switch it back to your preference in Character Configuration instead.
